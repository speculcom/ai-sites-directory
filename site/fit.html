这不是排名。每个类目取前 10 个常用入口，用一句话写清「什么时候该点它」。真正的规格、价格与许可仍在官网；拿不准就回目录按类目筛。This is not a ranking. The first ten common entries in each category get one line on when to open them. Specs, prices and licences stay on the official site; if unsure, go back to the directory and filter by category.

「适合」只描述任务形态，不代表能力更强或更便宜；同一任务往往有多个可选入口，先小范围试再扩大。“Fit” describes the shape of the task, not superior capability or lower price; most tasks have several plausible entries, so try small before scaling.
